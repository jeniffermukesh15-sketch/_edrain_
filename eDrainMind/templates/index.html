What does the crew plan do?

It turns the risk scores into a work order. It takes every Act now drain (or the Watch drains if none are red), puts the one that will overflow soonest first, and gives each stop to the crew that can get there earliest. Crews start at the Depot, drive about 15 km/h and spend 15 min cleaning each drain.

"after predicted overflow" means that crew will arrive later than the AI expects the drain to overflow. Press 2 or 3 crews to split the work and cut the waiting time.

Dispatch crews and clean is a simulation: it marks the planned drains as cleaned (waste cleared, water level halved, one more blockage recorded) so you can watch the risk fall. It does not contact real crews.
